Welcome to the AL-ARABI Education Management System Help Centre

Welcome

The Help & Support Centre provides guidance for using the AL-ARABI Education Management System. Whether you are an administrator, teacher, student, parent, or examination candidate, this page is designed to help you understand the platform and quickly resolve common issues.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I log in?

Use the email address and password provided by your institution or system administrator.

I forgot my password.

Use the "Forgot Password" option on the login page to securely reset your password.

I cannot access my examination.

Confirm that your examination link, access code, and scheduled examination time are correct.

My results are unavailable.

Results may remain hidden until they are officially released by the examination administrator.

Technical Support

If you experience technical issues such as login problems, examination errors, missing information, or unexpected system behaviour, please contact your institution's administrator. System administrators can review logs, verify permissions, and resolve technical issues securely.
