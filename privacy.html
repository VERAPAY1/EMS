Privacy Policy

AL-ARABI Education Management System (EMS)

Your privacy is important to us. AL-ARABI EMS is committed to protecting your personal information through modern security practices and responsible data management.

1. Introduction

AL-ARABI Education Management System (EMS) is designed to provide secure educational management services including examinations, attendance, academic records, administration, finance, library management, and communication. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, protect, and manage information entrusted to our platform.

2. Information We Collect

  • Personal Information
  • Academic Records
  • Attendance Records
  • Examination Results
  • User Login Information
  • Device Information
  • Activity Logs
  • System Security Logs

3. How We Use Information

The information collected is used exclusively for providing educational services, improving user experience, maintaining system security, generating reports, authenticating users, managing examinations, and ensuring the integrity of institutional operations.

4. Data Protection

AL-ARABI EMS implements encrypted communication, secure authentication, controlled database access, regular monitoring, and continuous security improvements to safeguard all user information against unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or destruction.

5. Confidentiality

Personal information is never intentionally shared with unauthorized individuals or organizations. Access is granted only to authorized personnel based on institutional roles and permissions.

6. Cookies

Our platform may use cookies and similar technologies to improve system performance, maintain secure sessions, and enhance user experience.

7. Third-Party Services

AL-ARABI EMS may utilize trusted third-party services such as secure cloud hosting, authentication providers, and database services. These providers operate under strict security and privacy standards.

8. User Responsibilities

  • Protect login credentials.
  • Use strong passwords.
  • Report suspicious activities immediately.
  • Keep account information accurate.

9. Data Retention

Educational records and related information are retained only for the period required by institutional policies, applicable regulations, and operational requirements.

10. Policy Updates

This Privacy Policy may be updated periodically to reflect improvements, legal requirements, and technological advancements. Users are encouraged to review this page regularly.

11. Contact

For questions regarding this Privacy Policy, users may contact the AL-ARABI EMS Administration through the official communication channels provided within the platform.
